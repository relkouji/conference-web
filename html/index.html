About The Seminar

The National Multidisciplinary Seminar SINDU Batch 2 – Special Edition Mother's Day: Women Contribution in All Sciences is an academic forum that highlights the important roles and contributions of women across various scientific disciplines. The seminar brings together researchers, academics, practitioners, and students to share research findings, insights, and innovative ideas in a multidisciplinary environment.

This seminar is free of charge for all participants and presenters, with accepted papers published in the SINDU2 Proceedings. Selected papers may also be considered for publication in partner journals, subject to journal review processes and additional requirements. Supported by LLDIKTI Region III (Jakarta), the seminar promotes inclusive collaboration and knowledge dissemination.

Registration Fee

Participation in this seminar is free of charge for all attendees.
However, presenters are required to pay a registration fee per accepted paper. The fee covers paper publication in the conference proceedings and seminar facilities.
