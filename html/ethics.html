Ethics and Malpractice Statement

GRACE 2026

The Proceedings of the International Conference on Governance, Resilience, Accountability, Corporate Sustainability, and Ethics (GRACE 2026) is a peer-reviewed open-access proceeding. We only accept and publish articles in the fields of accounting, finance, management, governance, sustainability, and resilient business.

The committee is responsible for the peer-review publication process. The following statements clarify the ethical behavior required by all parties: authors, editors, and peer-reviewers, adapted from the Committee of Publication Ethics (COPE).

Author Responsibilities

  • Authors must submit original work and ensure it has not been published or considered for publication elsewhere.
  • Authors must ensure their findings are based on honest work, free from fabrication, falsification, or plagiarism.
  • All listed authors must have made a significant contribution to the research and agreed to the final submission.
  • Authors must disclose any funding/grants and potential conflicts of interest.
  • If significant errors are identified post-submission, authors must promptly notify the committee.

Editor Responsibilities

  • Editors are responsible for the quality of the review process and the final publication decision (Accept/Reject/Revise).
  • Editors must perform initial screening and ensure reviewers possess suitable expertise.
  • Editors must maintain the confidentiality of submitted manuscripts and ensure no publication occurs without proper declarations.

Reviewer Responsibilities

  • Reviewers must treat manuscripts as strictly confidential and privileged information.
  • Reviews must be conducted objectively, providing constructive and clear feedback.
  • Reviewers must notify the editor of any conflict of interest and withdraw from the review process if necessary.
  • Reviews must be completed within the designated time frame.
