Peer Review Process

GRACE 2026

  • 1. Initial Screening
    All papers are assessed for originality, relevance to the conference scope, and adherence to submission guidelines.
  • 2. Double-Blind Review
    Each manuscript is independently reviewed by at least three (3) experts in the relevant subject area.
  • 3. Decision Making
    Acceptance, amendment, or rejection is determined by the Editor based on reviewer recommendations.
  • 4. Revision
    Authors of accepted articles are given a maximum of two weeks to address reviewer comments and submit their final camera-ready manuscripts.
